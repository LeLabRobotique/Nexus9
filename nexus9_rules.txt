NEXUS-9 RULES (GM reference)

CORE: Roll ATTRIBUTE DIE + SKILL DIE + MODS >= TN.
TN: 7 Easy | 9 Standard | 11 Hard | 13 Extreme | 15 Near-impossible. Default TN 9.
Fail (<TN): no intent, add consequence. Success (>=TN): intent achieved. Strong (>=TN+4): intent + benefit.
Strong benefits: faster, quieter/less Heat, better position, extra info, +2 dmg.
Mods: +2 strong advantage, +1 minor, -1 minor disadvantage, -2 strong disadvantage.
Help: +1 to roll, or helper rolls die: 4+ = +2, else +0.
Spike d20 (optional, rare): 1-3 complication even on success; 18-20 advantage even on fail.

ATTRIBUTES (d4-d12): Grit (endurance/willpower) | Reflex (speed/aim) | Mind (analysis/hacking/tech) | Face (persuasion/intimidation/deception).
SKILLS (d4-d12): Fight | Shoot | Move | Stealth | Hack | Observe | Talk | Threaten | Craft | Operate | Street | Med.
VIT = 10 + Grit die max. CHG = 6 + Mind die max. ARM = armor. Heat = city attention (0+).

PLAYBOOKS:
Riotline: Fight+1, Move+1; Grit+1 OR Reflex+1. Edge Break the Line (1 CHG): melee hit → Shove/Disarm/Pin.
Triggerhand: Shoot+1, Observe+1; Reflex+1 OR Face+1. Edge Clean Shot (1 CHG): ignore 2 cover penalty.
Ghostwalker: Stealth+1, Move+1; Reflex+1 OR Mind+1. Edge Vanish (1 CHG): stay hidden after acting.
Breaker: Hack+1, Observe+1; Mind+1 OR Reflex+1. Edge Chain Access (1 CHG): +2 next hack same system OR Heat-1.
Talkknife: Talk+1, Street+1; Face+1 OR Mind+1. Edge Turn the Ask (1 CHG): 1/scene Talk success→strong.
Wiredoc: Med+1, Craft+1; Mind+1 OR Grit+1. Edge Patch Protocol (1 CHG): heal 1d6 VIT + stop Bleeding, Med TN 9.

BACKGROUNDS: Streetborn (1 contact; Street+1, Stealth+1) | Corpo Ledger (access token; Talk+1, Observe+1) | Ghost-Runner (bolt-hole; Hack+1, Stealth+1) | Clinic Debt (free patch 1/session; Med+1, Grit+1) | Courier Loop (knows routes; Move+1, Observe+1) | Salvage Faith (tech intuition; Craft+1, Mind+1).

COMBAT: 2 Actions/round. Init: Reflex, highest first, PCs win ties.
Range: Close (melee) | Near (room) | Far (street) | Distant (sniper).
Melee: (Grit or Reflex)+Fight. Ranged: Reflex+Shoot. TN: 9 open | 11 cover/moving | 13 elite.
Cover: Light +2 TN, Heavy +4 TN, Exposed -2 TN.
Damage: weapon die - ARM → VIT. Strong: +2 dmg.

HARM: Down at VIT 0 (crawl/speak only). Injury trigger: VIT 0 OR single hit 8+ after ARM.
Injury (d12): 1-3 Bruised(-1 phys) | 4-6 Bleeding(-1 VIT/round) | 7-9 Shaken(-1 Mind/Face) | 10-11 Crippled(half move) | 12 Critical(death w/o Med).
Stabilize: Grit+Med or Mind+Med TN 9 → stop Bleeding/stabilize. Strong: +1d6 VIT.

HACKING: System = Firewall(TN), ICE(dmg die), Trace(Heat on fail).
Silent: TN+2, less fallout. Loud: normal TN, bigger, Heat spikes.
Actions: Probe(learn) | Bypass(doors/cams) | Hijack(drones) | Wipe(logs/-Heat) | Crash(shutdown/loud).
Fail (pick 1): Trace(+Heat) | ICE(dmg VIT/CHG) | Lockout(change approach) | Countermeasure(drones/alarms).
Presets: Street TN9/d6/+1 | Midline TN11/d8/+2 | Crown TN13/d10/+3.
Systems: Camera Net | Door Grid | Drone Bay | Comms Mesh | Finance Terminal | Medical System | Truth Recorder | Archive Node(high consequence).
Tags: Airgapped | Honeyed | Stale(-2 TN+glitches) | Biometric | Proxy-Wrapped.

HEAT: rises from loud fights, messy hacks, exposure. 3: patrols tighten. 6: drones/raids. 9: identity disruption/erasure.

SOCIAL: Talk(persuade/lie/negotiate) | Threaten(intimidate/coerce). TN: 9 open | 11 cautious | 13 protected | 15 elite.
Leverage(proof/money/favor): +2 or TN-2. Strong: extra info, secrecy, better terms, favor owed.
